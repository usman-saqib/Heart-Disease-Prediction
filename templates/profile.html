Your Heart Health Blueprint

Clinically‑backed habits to protect your most vital organ

Balanced Nutrition

Prioritise whole foods – fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, and whole grains. Limit saturated and trans fats.

Diet

Regular Exercise

At least 150 minutes of moderate activity or 75 minutes of vigorous activity per week strengthens your heart.

Fitness

Quit Smoking

Smoking is a leading cause of cardiovascular disease. Seek professional support to quit completely.

Habits

Limit Junk Food

Cut back on processed foods, sugary drinks, and deep‑fried items. Choose home‑cooked meals with fresh ingredients.

Nutrition

Control Salt & Sugar

Keep sodium under 2,300 mg/day and added sugars below 10% of daily calories to maintain healthy blood pressure.

Intake

Quality Sleep

Sleep 7–8 hours nightly. Poor sleep quality is linked to hypertension, diabetes, and heart disease.

Rest

Stress Management

Incorporate meditation, deep breathing, or yoga into your routine to lower cortisol and protect your heart.

Mental Health

Regular Check‑ups

Monitor blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood glucose annually. Early detection can save your life.
